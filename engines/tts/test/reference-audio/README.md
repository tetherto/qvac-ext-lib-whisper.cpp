# Reference audio fixtures

## `jfk.wav`

- Content: an 11-second excerpt of President John F. Kennedy's inaugural
  address, 20 January 1961.
- Exact source:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/v1.9.1/samples/jfk.wav
- Repository copy: matches the upstream whisper.cpp v1.9.1 sample
  byte-for-byte.
- Format: RIFF/WAVE, mono, 16-bit PCM, 16 kHz.
- SHA-256:
  `59dfb9a4acb36fe2a2affc14bacbee2920ff435cb13cc314a08c13f66ba7860e`.

Public-domain basis: the JFK Library catalog identifies the official
inaugural-address recording as public domain. The source is National Archives
and Records Administration Record Group 274.6, Records of the Joint Inaugural
Committee 1961:

https://www.jfklibrary.org/asset-viewer/archives/usg-17

The catalog states that the item has public-domain copyright status. It is a
work of the United States federal government prepared as part of official
duties and is therefore outside U.S. copyright under 17 U.S.C. 105.
